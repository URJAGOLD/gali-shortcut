🛣️ Gali Shortcut — Free Route Finder v2

100% FREE: OpenStreetMap + OSRM + Valhalla. No Google, no card, no API key. Hara mota route = gali shortcut, patli grey dash line = main road (sirf compare ke liye).

0 = Koi jam nahi
    ⚠️ v2 me kya badla: Pehle sirf badi-sadak band karne ki setting thi — kai routes par wo kaam hi nahi karti thi aur route main road wala hi reh jata tha. Ab app via-point baiting bhi karti hai: main route ke side me 500-600m andar mohalle ki sadak par point daal kar route ko zabardasti galiyon se ghumati hai, phir sabse alag aur sahi shortcut chun kar hara mota dikhati hai.

    Free stack me Google wala live traffic nahi hota — time sadak ki lambai/speed se hisaab hai. Jam slider tumhara andaza hai, live data nahi. Public free servers: ~1 request/second, halka personal use hi.
